Jenny — Pocket TTS predefined voice (voices/jenny.wav)

The voice's reference recording is a 26-second cut from the
jenny-tts-dataset, recorded and released by Jenny (dioco-group)
(https://github.com/dioco-group/jenny-tts-dataset). Pocket speaks in this
voice by conditioning on the recording; no model was trained on the
dataset for it. The dataset's attribution term dictates that the voice be
named "Jenny", and where practical "Jenny (Dioco)".


LICENCE

Verbatim from the dataset README:

    Attribution is required in software/websites/projects/interfaces
    (including voice interfaces) that generate audio in response to user
    action using this dataset. Atribution means: the voice must be referred
    to as "Jenny", and where at all practical, "Jenny (Dioco)". Attribution
    is not required when distributing the generated clips (although
    welcome). Commercial use is permitted. Don't do unfair things like claim
    the dataset is your own. No further restrictions apply.
